## 🌐 Enable GitHub Pages (Get Your Public Link)

### Step 1: Go to Settings
Visit: **https://github.com/Temm2/agents-docs/settings/pages**

### Step 2: Configure Pages
Under **"Source"** section:
1. Select: **"Deploy from a branch"**
2. Branch: **"main"**
3. Folder: **"/docs"** (important!)
4. Click **"Save"**

### Step 3: Wait 1-2 Minutes
GitHub will build and deploy your site.

### Step 4: Your Public Link
Your documentation will be live at:
```
https://temm2.github.io/agents-docs/
```
